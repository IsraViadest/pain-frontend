# P.A.I.N. / Little ideas, clear facts

Artist team | ELI5 explanation level | English | 9 September 2026

## The project

CARE / PEOPLE / PLACES. Personal And Interconnected with Nature. A globe bringing bodily
pain, feelings in writing, environmental change, and economic circumstances together.

Optional opening: "This globe helps us notice people and places that may need care."
The Earth is not literally a person feeling hurt. The layers do not make one suffering score.

## The four layers

BODIES: dents and dots make hidden difficulty visible. Six health categories; 15,282 mapped
observations. IHME and WorldPop. Dots are not patients.

WORDS: fourteen themes in writing. A visible word need not mean strong evidence, and cannot
describe everyone in a country.

SURROUNDINGS: red for warming; pale/green atmosphere for emissions. Berkeley Earth: 8,100
observations. Climate TRACE: 2,339. Cold water can warm and still be cold. The atmosphere
is an artistic shape, not measured smoke.

ECONOMY: stronger yellow means lower GDP per person. World Bank, 2024; 190 values, 16 gaps.
Production compared with population is not money equally shared, household income, or kindness.

## Current exhibition

249,438 texts: 5,000 news articles plus 244,438 other texts. This earlier selection supplies
the displayed words. For news, country means publisher origin, not event location.

MATCHING WORDS / NEARBY PLACES: Delaunay links make a triangle mesh. Gabriel is a sparser
comparison: another matching-category point inside a pair's diameter-circle prevents a connection. The
globe uses the three-dimensional idea. Lines do not show feelings spreading.

TURN / CHOOSE / NOTICE: rotation, country selection, filters, and composed sound direct
attention. A blank can mean missing information. No personal story is required.

## Latest PCAI research

ADULT BACKGROUND: about 600 million upstream CCNews articles; 393,745,301 prepared main-corpus
articles, including 388,842,327 dated 2015-2026. Clean text, remove duplicates, check dates,
mask detected identifiers, resolve publisher country. General news is not pain-keyword-filtered.

Verified completed analysis: 25,932,030 news articles plus 244,438 other texts = 26,176,468
records across 192 countries. Full-corpus classification is not established as started.

AI is designed for 100 languages. Its multilingual synthetic teaching collection has 573,143
examples; 333,236 fitted the adopted model. Training differs from reading new texts. Reliability
varies by language; a score is not a percentage of people.

## Optional framing

Clear sheets: different layers. Library pages: limited samples. Baskets made by a group:
production and group size, without assuming equal ownership. Fictional characters: a gentle
way to discuss feelings. These comparisons are choices to adapt, combine, or ignore.
