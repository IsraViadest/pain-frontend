# P.A.I.N. / Curious minds, clear connections

Artist team | Age-eight explanation level | English | 9 September 2026

## The project

PEOPLE / CARE / CONNECTION. Personal And Interconnected with Nature. Four kinds of information
share one globe. DATA means recorded information; an ESTIMATE uses evidence to work out a value;
a SAMPLE is a selection. The artwork brings concerns together without one overall pain score.

## The four layers

BODIES: dents and dots; six health categories; IHME and WorldPop; 15,282 mapped observations,
not patients. Imaginary towns: 100 and 1,000 people, each with 1 in 10 affected, means 10 and
100 affected. Same fraction, different count.

ENVIRONMENT: red shows warming; pale/green atmosphere represents emissions. Berkeley Earth:
8,100 observations; Climate TRACE: 2,339. Imaginary climate averages: 10 to 14 degrees is
4 degrees warmer; 25 to 26 is 1. Cooler can warm more. This is not today's weather or smoke.

ECONOMY: stronger yellow means lower GDP per person. World Bank, 2024; 190 values, 16 gaps.
Imaginary production: 12 units / 3 people = 4 per person on average; 20 / 10 = 2. The larger total
has the smaller average. Calculation does not mean equal ownership.

WORDS: fourteen distress categories. Several themes can coexist. Scores concern writing,
not the percentage of residents feeling something. Missing evidence differs from zero.

## Current exhibition

249,438 texts = 5,000 news articles + 244,438 other texts. News country means PUBLISHER ORIGIN.
Displayed language is an artist choice. Matching-category country labels form the DELAUNAY
triangle mesh. GABRIEL is a sparser comparison: another same-category point inside a pair's
diameter-circle blocks that mathematical connection; the globe uses a three-dimensional ball.
Lines show geographic arrangement, not feelings spreading. Rotation, filters, and composed
sound direct attention. No personal disclosure required.

## Latest PCAI research

100 LANGUAGES; MULTILINGUAL SYNTHETIC DATA. Constructed teaching examples, not testimony.
573,143 prepared; 333,236 fitted the model. Quotation, "not," and overlapping themes matter.
Training differs from classifying new texts; reliability differs across languages.

CCNEWS: about 600 million upstream; 393,745,301 prepared main-corpus articles, including
388,842,327 dated 2015-2026. Clean text, remove duplicates, check dates, mask detected
identifiers, resolve publisher country. General news, not pain-keyword-selected.

Verified completed: 25,932,030 news + 244,438 other texts = 26,176,468 across 192 countries.
Full-corpus production classification is not established as started.

## Optional framing

Clear sheets explain layers. Library shelves explain samples. Fictional characters explain
overlapping feelings. Made-up towns and production totals explain comparisons. Choose examples
that match the visitor's curiosity; no required wording or order.
