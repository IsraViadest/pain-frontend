# P.A.I.N. / Advanced quick reference

Artist team | Professor level | English | 9 September 2026

## The project

Personal And Interconnected with Nature. The Personal-Planetary-Pain Map relates bodily burden,
distress in language, environmental pressure, and economic circumstances. Keywords: construct,
indicator, representation, interdependence, care. The layers do not establish one unit of suffering.

## The four layers

PHYSICAL: IHME Global Burden of Disease with WorldPop. Six conditions; 15,282 condition-points;
13,183 assigned to 155 countries. Scars and dots represent burden, not individual patients.
Country symbols emphasize local maxima. Exact health measure and year remain unconfirmed.

ENVIRONMENTAL: Berkeley Earth, 8,100 temperature-change observations; Climate TRACE,
2,339 emissions observations. Country-assigned support: 140 and 146 countries respectively.
Keywords: change versus heat; emissions versus exposure; local maximum versus national total.
Selected periods, temperature baseline, and emissions measure remain unconfirmed.

SOCIOECONOMIC: World Bank GDP per capita, 2024, current US dollars. 190 values; 16 missing.
Inverse logarithmic yellow: lower output, stronger signal. GDP is not income, distribution, or welfare.

EMOTIONAL: mean classifier scores from country-associated texts. Publisher geography differs from
event location and residence. Words invite recognition; they do not diagnose populations.

## Current exhibition

249,438 texts: 5,000 news plus 244,438 expressions. Where both exist, family means receive
75/25 weighting. Anger leads in 152 of 192 countries. No-pain/out-of-scope controls are excluded
from visible winners and exceed the winner in 121 countries.

192 displayed labels use 101 language codes and 20 scripts, separately from article languages.

## Latest PCAI research

Classifier designed for 100 languages; multilingual synthetic collection: 573,143 examples.
Adopted v9 fitted on 333,236. Frozen BGE-M3; 1,024-unit hidden layer; 512-token view; 70 outputs:
3 scope, 14 broad, 47 fine, 2 safety, 4 out-of-scope reasons. Coverage is not equal accuracy.

Macro-F1, broad/fine: clean synthetic .9028/.7772; stress .6658/.6552. Keywords: scope gating,
threshold, calibration, domain shift, test reuse, effective sample size. Real-world validity remains open.

CCNews: approximately 600M upstream; 393,745,301 prepared country-linked records, including
388,842,327 dated 2015-2026. Filters: normalization, dates, duplicates, privacy, publisher attribution.
Verified completed: 25,932,030 news + 244,438 expressions = 26,176,468. Record-equal pooling;
99.07% news globally; anger leads in 178/192. Full-corpus production classification is unverified.

## Optional framing

"Which observation supports this interpretation?" "Whose experience reaches this source?"
"What changes when we change the weighting?" Connection can be artistically meaningful while
causal inference remains unresolved. These are optional starting points, without required wording.
