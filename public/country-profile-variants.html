Interactive emotion subsets

On Emotional Pain, each legend name has a small ×. Faint means included; stronger means excluded. Click × again to restore it. Clicking the name selects a network; clicking × reranks countries across the enabled categories. Country selection and camera remain while its terms and network update.

This works across cp presets. The legend is hidden in all the pain. Categories with no winning countries cannot select a network but can still be included or excluded. Source scores are unchanged. Excluding anger does not hide all anger countries: each gets its strongest remaining category.

The small legend title reads “Strongest captured emotion by country”. Each category shows its current country count, including countries beyond the visible hemisphere. Counts recalculate when filters change. Zero-country names are dimmed and cannot select a network. Excluded names are struck through; their × remains available to restore them.

Share exclusions with cpExclude, a comma-separated list of category keys. An explicit empty cpExclude= enables all, including v44-b. With every category excluded, emotional labels/networks disappear and the profile says “filtered out”. Truly missing observations retain “no data”.

  • New data, anger excluded: Every valid country chooses its strongest remaining pain category. This produces new category memberships and therefore new networks.
  • New data, only Hurt and Shame: Only Hurt and Shame compete for every valid country. The original category scores remain unchanged; this is a subset exploration rather than a new dataset.
  • Explicitly restore all emotions: The empty exclusion parameter enables all 14 categories, including when opening the preset whose initial choice excluded anger.

Meaning and limits

Combined emotion v2 uses CCNews75% / expressions25% (5 September2026):192 valid countries, three missing. With all14 emotions enabled,152 countries have anger as winner. The new fonts cover eligible native terms.

GDP/person uses World Bank2024 current US dollars,187 countries. Lower GDP/person is stronger yellow through an inverted log display, an artistic socioeconomic proxy rather than a direct pain measurement. Missing remains missing.

Ocean contours show the artistic scar field, not measured bathymetry. Dot diameter and atmospheric volume are rendering treatments. Physical-phone acceptance is deferred. Older URLs use today's common correctness fixes; they are not archived application builds.
